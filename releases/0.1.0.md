## 中文

下载下面的 **GlanceDrift-win-x64.zip**，解压后运行 **GlanceDrift.exe**。把鼠标放到想看的地方，按住 **F8** 试试。移动鼠标可以换取景位置，上滚看细节，下滚看更大范围，松手就收起来。

有两块屏幕的话，把鼠标移到副屏上的一行小字，再按 F8。预览会出现在你刚才打字的屏幕上。双击托盘图标可以换快捷键，也可以把浮窗固定在右下角。

## English

Grab **GlanceDrift-win-x64.zip** below, unzip it, and run **GlanceDrift.exe**. Point at something and hold **F8** for a live peek. Move the pointer to look around, scroll up for detail, or scroll down to see more. Let go when you're done.

Got two monitors? Point at a line of small text on the other screen and hold F8. The preview appears on the screen where you were typing. Double-click the tray icon to pick your own shortcut or pin the preview to a corner. The English README has a short guide to the Chinese menu labels.
